3. I have successfully implemented the backend workflows for refunds, enrollment updates, and WhatsApp notifications.

Key Workflow Enhancements:
Automated Refund Logic:

Regular Classes & Courses: Implemented Pro-rata refunds. When a class is ended early or a holiday is declared with the "Refund" option, the system calculates the exact value of the remaining sessions or missed dates relative to the student's paid cycle.
Workshops: On cancellation, the system now automatically triggers a Full Refund for all participants.
Free Trials & Partial Payments: The system identifies trial users and skips financial refunds for them while still maintaining their enrollment updates.
Smart Enrollment Sync:

Automatic Initialization: Every new booking now calculates an initial enrollment_end_date (1 month for regular classes, or the course/workshop specific date).
Dynamic Extensions: If you select "Extend Enrollment" during a holiday declaration, the system calculates and saves the new end date (e.g., +1 week) for every active student.
Cancellation Sync: Ending a class early automatically updates all student enrollment end dates to that specific date, moving them to "Inactive" once that date passes.
Advanced Notifications & Logging:

Detailed WhatsApp Messages: Students now receive precise information, such as the exact refund amount or the specific new date their enrollment ends.
Operational Logs: All refunds, extensions, and notifications are now logged in the system (writable/logs/) with booking IDs and calculation details for your reference.
